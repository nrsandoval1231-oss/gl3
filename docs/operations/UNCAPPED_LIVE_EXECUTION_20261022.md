## Scope of the proof

This proves genuine advisory Jev materiality and Sol multi-document load/buyer execution on the covered retained cases, with uncapped USD policy and persistent receipts. It does not prove universal identity accuracy, a real JCL-to-Sabey association, accepted buyer authority, a new automatic production card refresh, scheduled cloud operation, or launch completion. Legacy worker compatibility is separately covered by deterministic tests. Prior UI/performance evidence is retained with its original candidate identity.

Read-only production inspection found Jev disabled, reasoning model `gpt-6-luna`, a $0.01 per-call maximum and $50 daily/monthly global budgets. Existing OpenAI credentials successfully retrieved the `gpt-6.1-sol` model. Production rollout requires the separate merge/deploy authority specified in the owner directive.
