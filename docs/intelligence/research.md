# GL3-015C bounded research question

GL3-015C adds a `research` kind to the existing PostgreSQL `jobs` queue. Ingestion jobs keep their required source reference; research jobs have no synthetic `Source` row and carry one bounded question in `research_jobs`. The question is unique per power need and target fact, capped at one official URL, one HTTP request, 20 seconds, and zero spend.

The deterministic planner currently supports one evidence-backed pair: the accepted Taylor Project Comal Signal from article 2032, including project name and 1051 CR 401. Once that Signal is accepted with its current immutable Evidence and Interpretation, ingestion automatically enqueues the bounded developer question in the same transaction. Queue insertion failures roll back the accepted update while previously committed raw source evidence remains available for review. Research retrieval stays in the separately leased worker and never runs inline with source ingestion. Replaying ingestion does not create another job. Other Signals, identity conflicts, withdrawn records, and Signals with a known owner/developer do not enter this planner.

Eligibility is shared by queue creation and the opportunity projection. An unqueued eligible Signal reports that research is not queued and explains that acceptance queues it automatically. An unsupported Signal reports research as unavailable/UNKNOWN and states the missing identity-linked evidence; it is not presented with an action that will fail.

The opportunity projection distinguishes a cited Owner/Developer `ProjectRole` from identity facts that lack that citation. A cited role is shown as known with its supporting research evidence, even when the role was recorded against another need for the same project. An identity fact without a role is labeled recorded but unverified and directs the operator to review an official project-specific source. Neither state creates a duplicate research job or invents a citation.

The question asks which organization is the documented developer and allows only the City of Taylor's project page at `https://taylortx.gov/1383/KDC-Project-Comal`. The source page must identify Comal, the same County Road 401 site, and an explicit developer statement. A company mention alone cannot satisfy the check.

Fetched page evidence is retained separately from source-ingestion `Evidence`, with official URL, publisher, page date when supplied, retrieval timestamp, exact supporting excerpt/location, SHA-256 response hash, evidence label, basis, and confidence. Supported relationships link an `Organization`, `ProjectRole`, `PowerNeed`, and `Project`. The Signal tier, power facts, supply, and lifecycle remain untouched. A fetched page that does not support the relationship is retained as `Unknown`; a source failure persists an unresolved reason and next action. Repeated queue requests return the same job. An unresolved result can be retried up to the existing five-attempt fence limit, with each earlier outcome kept in the job history.

`/api/opportunities` exposes research status, one specific next action, and evidence-backed organizations. The authenticated POST endpoint remains available for an explicit retry, but accepted eligible ingestion is the normal queue trigger and needs no API call. No generic search, LLM, paid source, contacts, or outreach are used. Additional questions and source families require new bounded packets and official identity-linked access proof.
