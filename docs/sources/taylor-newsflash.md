# Taylor CivicPlus NewsFlash source

This accepted local source reads the City of Taylor's public Development News (category 30), Development Notices (category 31), and the bounded rolling archive interval configured in the source registry. The configured 59-day lookback plus 7-day overlap produces a 66-day interval ending on the retrieval date. Tests can supply a fixed cutoff date. It follows article links rendered by those pages and uses only the observed `archCc=2`, date, count, and offset archive controls.

The worker requires consistent archive totals, contiguous page bounds, complete row counts, a disabled terminal Next Page control, unique `(article_id, arcId)` pairs, and a repeated first-page identity set. A malformed, unstable, oversized, empty, or login response fails the job. Archive revisions are separate observations keyed by article and `arcId`; raw response hashes, source publication dates, archive dates, and retrieval/evidence dates retain distinct meanings. The rolling interval overlaps prior daily runs; there is no upstream snapshot token, so this is a stable client-side enumeration check, not atomic snapshot proof.

The parser selects the visible `main h1.article-header-title`, `.article-content`, and the single `.article-badge` in the article header; the badge is read separately from its posted date and the related-news footer. Missing or ambiguous header badges fail closed. Archive pagination and identity checks cover every row before category admission, and every bounded archive revision is retained with its exact badge label, source hash, and separately labeled archive dates. The concise evidence locator is `header/body; category: <badge>` with `; table dates` for archive records; it is checked against the existing 100-character evidence column bound without truncation. The article URL, article/revision identity, and article source hash remain in existing evidence fields. Mixed-category archive pages do not fail because unrelated items appear. The exact labels `Development News` and `Development Notices` are the only promotable categories; `News` and all other explicit labels remain non-target observations and cannot produce a Signal. The interpreter may create a Signal only when a non-withdrawn record has an exact target category, identifiable project name, dated publisher record, and one body sentence that binds a data center to a Taylor/Williamson site label or explicit road address. An address plus the configured City of Taylor source geography is retained as an attributed site, not a power claim. It creates no MW, load, supply, buyer, cancellation, or Prospect/Lead claims.

Withdrawn revisions are retained as separate evidence. The Taylor-specific worker links a withdrawn revision to a prior Signal only when the same source and numeric article ID lead to exactly one existing Signal need supported by an accepted earlier revision. The withdrawal creates one material change and appears as the latest planning-application state in the API/card timeline. Earlier positive evidence remains historical. This does not assert that the whole project was cancelled; campus viability and power impact remain UNKNOWN. Missing or ambiguous matches remain unlinked with reason UNKNOWN. The registry entry is accepted ACTIVE locally after current ordinary-worker ingestion, unchanged replay and authenticated browser proof. Deployed daily cadence remains unproven.

Taylor project identity uses the numeric article ID while each archive `arcId` remains a separate observation and evidence version. Accepted revisions of one article reuse one project and need only when prior accepted article evidence supports a unique project and the article text does not introduce conflicting project labels or distinct site addresses. In-place changes to a category-page observation are checked against all retained prior Evidence for that article before a Project or Signal can change. Conflicting versions are retained as evidence, their prior accepted interpretation remains in assessment history, and the changed version is marked UNKNOWN without project rename or lifecycle advancement. Ambiguous article-level identity remains UNKNOWN and creates no additional Signal. Separate numeric article IDs are not merged based only on a shared project name or city. Publication dates come only from the article header or head publication metadata; dates found in unrelated body, sidebar, or related-news content do not qualify.

This is technical public retrieval evidence only. It does not determine terms, copyright, robots policy, broader republication rights, request limits, cost, or update latency. See [the access contract](taylor-access-contract.md) for the observed fixed interval and its limitations.
