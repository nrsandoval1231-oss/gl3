# GL3-023 — Dallas, El Paso and Houston permits

Checked 2026-10-01 using a plain `GridLens public source reader/1.0` user agent. Route results, time stamps, query counts and source hashes are in [the research receipt](../../.agent/artifacts/GL3-023/SOURCE_RESEARCH.json). Raw workbooks and response bodies are ignored under `.artifacts/GL3-023/`.

## Dallas

The official [Reports page](https://dallascityhall.com/departments/sustainabledevelopment/Pages/Reports.aspx) publishes monthly Com-ResNew Excel workbooks. The official [report-generation guide](https://dallascityhall.com/departments/sustainabledevelopment/DCH%20documents/GeneratingReportsonBuildingPermitsdoc.version1.0.0.pdf) describes public Building → Reports → Building Issued, Submitted or Active report generation without a sign-in. The adapter acquires the linked monthly reports directly and selects only rows whose Record Type is `Commercial New Construction Permit`. It retains stable Record ID, Permit Issued Date, address, use, work type, area, cost, contact type/name, and the LP Business Name as an unverified role. Revised rows across overlapping monthly workbooks retain the newest report snapshot; evidence version history keeps earlier content.

The three retrieved workbooks (June, July, August 2026) held 95 qualifying source rows and 89 distinct Record IDs after six repeated IDs. Issue dates range from 2026-06-01 to 2026-08-28. The parser does not label this complete Dallas permit coverage: the dataset is monthly and does not cover all alterations or electrical records. The report page’s August link is the latest available; a September placeholder is not treated as a dataset. The legacy Dallas building-permit open-data dataset is discontinued and is not enabled. The guide documents public date-ranged Building Issued/Submitted/Active reports without sign-in, but interactive DallasNow execution was not verified as reproducible acquisition and is not automated. No separately published electrical, generator, or temporary-service route was verified for reproducible acquisition.

The source Record Type says Commercial for the selected rows, while five Proposed Land Use values identify residential occupancy. Those remain rejected by admission. Other accepted Signals arise from explicit proposed uses such as warehouse, hospital, light manufacturing, medical/scientific laboratory, and water treatment plant. These permit events support early construction Signals only; they do not prove data-center activity, temporary-power demand, construction status, load, schedule, rental fit, or buying authority. LP Business Name is not equated with the separately listed Contact Name or assumed to be the applicant’s employer.

## El Paso

The official [NewCommercial FeatureServer layer](https://gis.elpasotexas.gov/arcgis/rest/services/Planning/NewCommercial/FeatureServer/0) is readable without sign-in and advertises pagination up to 1,000 records. The adapter validates layer metadata, full-layer and 90-day counts, ordered OBJECTID pages, and the newest issue-date query. At the 2026-10-01 check, the layer had 11,391 records; the issue window returned 68 features and 67 distinct B1_ALT_ID permits (one identical repeated permit row). The observed issue-date range was 2026-08-04 through 2026-09-29. All 68 raw B1_APPL_ST values in the query were blank. The one-character Status field is geocoding status and is never mapped to permit lifecycle. B1_ALT_ID is the stable permit identity; OBJECTID remains provenance only.

The layer is limited to new commercial permits. The observed descriptions include short labels such as “Emerus Lee Trevino” and “2 CAR PARKING GARAGE BUILDING”; those do not establish an activity-specific commercial facility project. The current 67 stable permit rows therefore produce no Signals. This is an admission outcome, not an access failure or a reason to invent activity. NewCommercial is not all El Paso permits or citywide electrical coverage. A dated issued-permit event may support a Signal when its activity is independently supported even if current lifecycle is blank; no current work or rental need is inferred.

## Houston

The [Sold Permits page](https://www.houstonpermittingcenter.org/sold-permits-search), [council report](https://cohtora.houstontx.gov/approot/soldpermits/cc1001ah.htm), [public three-year search](https://cohtora.houstontx.gov/approot/soldpermits/online_permit.htm), and [permitting guide](https://www.houstonpermittingcenter.org/media/1881/download?attachment=) are publicly readable. The guide describes Sold Permit Search under issued building permits; the council report describes permits by district and week of sale. The weekly XLSX therefore proves a dated Sold (approved) permit event, not current permit status or active construction. Publication date remains unknown and distinct from the sale date.

The four latest weekly reports observed on 2026-10-01 contained 1,543 rows: 1,422 Building Pmt, 112 Demolition, and 9 OCC-BLDG PMT. The latest listing, Sept 7-13, points to `Sept 7-13.xlsx`; it contains 317 rows, with sale dates Sept 8-13 and workbook cells A2/A3 reading `From: 2026/09/07` and `To : 2026/09/14`. The weekly range convention treats the To date as exclusive, though the workbook does not mark that convention. Its core properties say created and modified 2026-09-14T16:43:22Z. These are internal report dates/metadata, not verified publication time. On Oct 1, the latest sale date was 18 calendar days old and the report-period end 17 days old; actual publication timestamp and lag remain UNKNOWN. The six weekly fields do not include party or contact fields.

The public exact-project search can retrieve detail rows without signing in. For `Building Pmt`, the observed selector maps to `PT=13`; detail acquisition is accepted only when the returned project number, date, and address match the weekly row. The detail fields preserve Buyer, combined Owner/Occupant, buyer address, and phone as separate source roles. Buyer’s person/company kind, phone ownership/type, current project assignment, and authority remain UNKNOWN unless separately supported. A listed person is not promoted to a verified buyer or decision-maker.
