# Signal Source Matrix

Canonical acquisition roadmap for GL3-015. Status describes **current GL3 acquisition**, not the existence of public records. `ACTIVE` means enabled locally with retrieval, persistence and replay proof; `PARTIAL` means selected places or event types; `PLANNED` means a plausible public route without an accepted adapter; `BLOCKED` means a known access or integrity obstacle; `NOT YET RESEARCHED` means no source contract has been checked. None implies nationwide coverage. The governing unit is a physical project **event**, not a permit type.

Pipeline: **source → observation → retained evidence → conservative project match → lifecycle event → Signal-worthiness**. Ingest a record without necessarily surfacing a Signal. Preserve dated revisions and negative events. Names alone never merge sites. An approval, generator rating, requested load or drilling authorization never establishes a temporary-power gap.

`Priority` is ordinal: **P1** next acquisition candidate, **P2** useful follow-up, **P3** research first. Cadence is a target or upstream publication pattern, not a verified SLA. The fields below specify what to seek and retain; they do not assert every listed source exposes them.

## Source interpretation and matching contract

- **Signal-worthy:** dated, linked, identifiable physical site/project plus a concrete lifecycle action and a plain explanation of why that action merits future power investigation. A permit class by itself is insufficient. A repair, routine Title V renewal or generic tariff is retained as an observation but normally not surfaced.
- **Match:** exact application/facility/parcel linkage or strong same-site evidence plus compatible project identity. Retain each observation and its revision. Record ambiguous candidates without merging. Addressless company news should not consolidate sites by name alone.
- **History:** show new projects and meaningful changes to known projects. Do not reannounce an unchanged record. A withdrawal, denial, cancellation or occupancy event can alter the lifecycle interpretation without deleting earlier evidence.
- **Unknown:** MW, energization dates, buyer, utility adequacy and temporary-power requirement remain `UNKNOWN` unless the cited source actually says them. Reported generator capacity and requested grid load are distinct from project operating load.

## Public research anchors

- [Texas RRC research queries](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/research-queries/) list W-1, completions, P-4 and production searches; [well-record guidance](https://www.rrc.texas.gov/oil-and-gas/research-and-statistics/obtaining-commission-records/oil-and-gas-well-records-online/) describes W-1/W-2/G-1/P-4 contents.
- [ERCOT Large Load Integration](https://www.ercot.com/services/rq/large-load-integration/) describes its process, not an open project-level feed.
- [EPA construction NOI guidance](https://www.epa.gov/npdes/submitting-notice-intent-noi-notice-termination-not-or-low-erosivity-waiver-lew-under) and [EPA air permit system](https://www.epa.gov/nsr/caaps) describe the permitting families; public acquisition contracts still need source-by-source proof.
- [FERC eLibrary guidance](https://www.ferc.gov/elibrary-quick-help) establishes docket identifiers useful for fixed infrastructure, subject to a separate acquisition proof.
