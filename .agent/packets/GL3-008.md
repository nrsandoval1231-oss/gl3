## Interpretation and identity

Build a permit-specific deterministic evaluator that uses structured class/work/status, description, address/city, and the cited permit row. A visible Signal requires a dated official permit, identifiable site/project or applicant, and a concrete construction, expansion, conversion, or commissioning activity with an explained reason to investigate activity-specific power. An issued building permit is evidence of permitted work, not proof work started, finished, or needs temporary power. Applications, withdrawn/canceled/expired permits, maintenance, ordinary interior tenant changes, and vague rows remain inbox/rejected or separately labeled without automatic Signal admission.
