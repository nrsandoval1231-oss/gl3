## Objective

Reduce repeated browse cards and replace bureaucratic permit headings with concise titles describing the supported work or event. Keep every underlying opportunity and source permit individually accessible; do not merge or delete database records, infer identity from address/name alone, invent named organizations, or hide record totals.

## Observed state and decision

Controller-provided authenticated API baseline `.artifacts/GL3-010/baseline-api.json` contains 50 active Signal rows. Nineteen Austin permit rows share the same `event-power` activity, exact full address `2100 BARTON SPRINGS RD, AUSTIN, TX`, and identical supported-action description naming Zilker Park plus explicit October 2–4 and 9–11, 2026 event dates. Present these as one evidence-backed browse group titled `Zilker Park music stages, vendors & hospitality · Oct 2026`, with all 19 original rows and their IDs/evidence individually accessible. This yields 32 browse cards and continues to report 50 underlying records.

Grouping is presentation-only and requires permit-source equality, exact normalized full location, exact activity, exact normalized reported action, and an explicit named place/event plus a concrete year/date in that action. Address-only, name-only, category-only, similar descriptions, different dates/years, or different activity never group. Unmatched and ambiguous items stay separate. Search is applied over retained rows while preserving a group's context and member navigation.

Permit display titles come from source-supported work/event descriptions after deterministic removal of permit IDs, administrative boilerplate, and leading date/status text. Preserve phase/building distinctions. If a useful title cannot be derived safely, use a neutral activity + location title and retain the permit identifier in member/evidence metadata. Non-permit project titles stay unchanged.
